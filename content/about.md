I am a PhD student in [Biostatistics](https://www.public-health.uiowa.edu/biostat/) at the University of Iowa, where I am receiving rigorous training in statistics and gaining valuable research experience through collaborations with researchers from other disciplines. I work as a research assistant in the Biostatistics Core at the [Holden Comprehensive Cancer Center](https://holden.uihealthcare.org/research/mogs-and-spores/neuroendocrine-tumors-spore). I have been fortunate to receive guidance from Drs. [Emily Roberts](https://emilykroberts.github.io/), [Patrick Breheny](https://pbreheny.org/web/), [Gideon Zamba](https://www.public-health.uiowa.edu/faculty/gideon-zamba/) and [David Zahrieh](https://www.mayo.edu/research/faculty/zahrieh-david-m-ph-d/bio-20528095).

My undergraduate major was Financial Engineering (within the field of Economics), and I previously worked as a quantitative researcher, focusing on high-frequency trading using methods such as stochastic analysis and reinforcement learning. Over time, I realized that I was more interested in applying statistical methods to address biomedical problems.

My current research interests include **Clinical Trials** (dose selection/optimization, seamless designs, and patient-focused drug development), **Bayesian Methods** (Bayesian adaptive designs and Bayesian borrowing), and **Causal Inference** (indirect comparisons, [covariate adjustment](https://carswg.github.io/), and [surrogate endpoint validation](https://www.surrogatemarkers.com/methods)). Some of these topics are related to my previous/current work or will be explored in my PhD dissertation research.
